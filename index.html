HOW TO PLAY

SLALOM

Six courses of single poles. Tap left and right in a steady rhythm to build speed, hold longer for a tighter carve. Pass every pole on its outside — go the wrong side and you are disqualified.

Later courses bring rhythm changes, hairpins, vertical flushes, delay gates and lines that wander across the hill.

DOWNHILL

Four tracks in a chase-cam view. Curves push you wide, so hold left or right to carve. Both thumbs together pull you into a tuck for top speed — 140 km/h and more.

Hit the safety fence and it grinds you down; miss a gate and your run is over.

CONTROLS

PhoneTouch the left or right half of the screen (the LEFT / RIGHT pads). Both thumbs = tuck.
Keyboard← → or A D steer · ↓ / SPACE tuck · ESC pause
MouseClick and hold the left or right half.

Slalom plays upright, downhill sideways — on a phone the game asks you to turn it.

Tracks are fictional tributes to the sport's famous descents — steep walls, long glides, blind jumps.

COINS, SKIERS & GEAR

Every finish pays coins — more for the podium, a personal best or a new medal. Spend them on six skiers with their own speed, edge grip, rhythm and airtime, on better skis, suits and edges, and on unlocking harder courses. Three difficulty levels, from Relaxed to Legend, change the rival field and the payout. Everything is earnable in play; there is nothing to buy.

FAQ

Is Pixel Ski free?

Yes. There are no purchases. Optional ads can double your coin payout or let you continue once after a disqualification — you can always say no.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device (local storage). Clearing your browser data or using a private window starts you from zero. Progress in the browser and in the app are separate.

Which browsers work?

Current Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox with WebGL. On phones, use it full screen: slalom upright, downhill sideways.

There is no sound.

Browsers only allow sound after your first tap or key press. Check the SOUND and MUSIC switches on the title screen and, on iPhone, the silent switch.
